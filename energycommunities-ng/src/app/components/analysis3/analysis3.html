Energy Community Analysis 3 Dashboard

For each member, given a set of production and consumption profiles, our app applies a decision-making algorithm to select the optimal production and consumption profile for each member, in order to optimize the shared energy within the energy community.

In this analysis, you can select an initial set of members (default community), choose who to remove and who to add, and compare the resulting communities:

  • Default: members initially selected
  • Wanted: selected members after applying your add/remove choices
  • Optimal: the best combination computed by the algorithm
