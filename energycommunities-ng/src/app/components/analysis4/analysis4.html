Optimal Battery Assignment

For each energy community, given a set of candidate batteries and a predefined budget, our app runs an optimization algorithm that selects which producers should be equipped with batteries and dynamically schedules charge/discharge operations. The goal is to maximize the shared energy within the community while minimizing the total investment. In addition, the system provides a cost analysis to estimate the payback time of the investment.
